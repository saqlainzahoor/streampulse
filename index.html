What is One Health?

One Health is the idea that human health, animal health, and ecosystem health are connected — a change in one shows up in the others. A stormwater outfall's water quality doesn't just affect fish: it affects the dog that swims below it, and the people at the park next to it. StreamPulse exists to make that chain visible, one reading at a time.

Why citizen science matters

Agencies can't monitor every stream every day. Volunteers who return to the same spot, again and again, are what turn a handful of readings into a trend anyone can trust — and a trend is what catches a problem before it becomes a crisis.
